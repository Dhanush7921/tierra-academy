# Design references

Supplied by the client as **inspiration only**. No branding, artwork or layouts were copied.
Large source videos are kept locally in `design-references/videos/` (git-ignored); representative
frames (1 fps) are committed in `design-references/frames/`.

| Reference | What we took (as principles) | How Tierra Academy expresses it |
|---|---|---|
| `ref-01-scroll-story-site` — a farm/egg brand site with a scroll-driven story | One hero object that "becomes" the story as you scroll; huge confident headlines with one highlighted word; section changes signalled by colour shifts; product cards with toggles | The **ESP32 board** assembles itself in the hero; the scroll flies a camera through six discipline **stations**; headline "Learn. Build. *Automate.*" with the gradient word; catalogue chips/toggles. All farming/egg concepts replaced with electronics, robotics, IoT, embedded, electrical and mechanical engineering. |
| `ref-02-glassy-dashboard` — dark, warm, frosted-glass CRM | Frosted glass cards over a deep background, orange accents, icon sidebar, KPI tiles with glow, pill bar charts, donut | Admin + student panels: `.glass` tokens, orange/amber glow accents, KPI `StatCard`s, SVG bar/area/donut charts, glass sidebar. |
| `ref-03-3d-office-dashboard` — isometric 3D office with floating labels | A miniature isometric room as navigation, floating name-tag labels anchored to 3D objects | The **student "engineering lab"**: laptop → My Courses, ESP32 → Embedded, robot arm → Robotics, wall display → Live Classes, bench → Resources, frame → Certificates, with accessible HTML labels projected from 3D anchors. |

The Tierra Automation logo (`images/tierra-automation-logo.jpg`) is used exactly as supplied — never recoloured;
on dark surfaces it sits on its own white disc.
