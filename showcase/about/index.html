The academy

Tierra Academy — Learn. Build. Automate.

Tierra Academy is the learning platform of Tierra Automation, an engineering and technology company based in Visakhapatnam, India.

What we teach

Practical, project-first courses in six disciplines: Electronics, Robotics, the Internet of Things, Embedded Systems, Electrical Engineering and Mechanical Engineering. Every course is designed around building real things — circuits, firmware, connected devices, mechanisms and automated systems.

How you learn

  • Live classes — scheduled, instructor-led sessions with attendance and recordings for enrolled students.
  • Recorded courses — secure streaming lessons you can resume on any device.
  • Hybrid courses — recorded fundamentals plus live build sessions.
  • Assessments — quizzes and assignments, with verifiable certificates when you complete the requirements.
